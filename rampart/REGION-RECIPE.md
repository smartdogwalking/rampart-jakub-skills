# Region evidence recipe

Use this record for one named interface effect. Explain the mechanism before proposing a reproduction.

- Target: source URL, region and source version or retrieval date.
- Question: the exact effect or behavior being studied.
- Inspection: viewport, input, visited states and actual browser/source evidence.
- Layers: paint order, elements, pseudo-elements, media and clipping or compositing relationships.
- Mechanism: what each layer does and why the relationship produces the observed result.
- Values: measurements with units; calculations with their inputs; separate reconstruction choices.
- Time: initial, active, interrupted and settled behavior when inspected. A still does not establish these states.
- Evidence: measured, derived or inferred for each material claim.
- Transfer: the relationship worth reusing, required assets and behavior, and what depends on the source's geometry or identity.
- Unknowns: inaccessible styles, canvas/shader internals, unvisited states and unverified timings.

Screenshot-only analysis is a reconstruction. Compiled styles do not identify the author's original library or source code by themselves.

For a Rampart dossier, map measured evidence to Observed with its actual state. Mark calculations as Sampled or estimated with their derivation. Keep inferred source behavior Unknown. Label original reconstruction decisions Chosen.
